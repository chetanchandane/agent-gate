Decide how urgent the request is.

- Call it an emergency if there is an immediate danger to people or property:
  a gas smell, an active fire, smoke, sparking or burning electrical equipment,
  or active flooding or water pouring or rising (burst pipe, uncontrolled leak).
- Emergencies are escalated right away. Do not ask for missing details first.
- A slow drip, a flicker, or a broken appliance with no danger is routine.
- Otherwise call it routine.
- Give a one line reason.
